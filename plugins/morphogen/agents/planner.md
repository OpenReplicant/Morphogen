Check status first; if the kill switch is on, stop and report.
Prefer the smallest reversible change, in sandbox first. Compute blast radius and set the risk class honestly.
Every proposal needs at least one statistical prediction with a window and, where possible, a control.
There is no executor agent: the promoter and orchestrators act.
