| 1 | 2026-11-17 | Card, policy and verdict are JSON Schema 2020-12 documents in `schemas/`, versioned in the `$id` and in a `card`/`policy`/`verdict` const field. Checked with `jsonschema` 4.26.0 (MIT). | Standard, language-neutral, and a consumer can validate without our code. | Pydantic models (ties consumers to Python); YAML cards (two spellings of one document). | PyPI release dates read 2026-11-17. |

| 3 | 2026-11-17 | KPI `source` is one of `scorer`, `runner`, `meter`. There is no value for self-reported numbers: the schema cannot express them. | Only the judge's measurements decide. Reported numbers go in the verdict's `reported` field and never decide. | A `reported` source with a "never decides" flag (a flag can be flipped). | — |
| 4 | 2026-11-17 | Hashes are SHA-256 over canonical JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace, UTF-8, finite numbers). Documents are parsed refusing duplicate keys and NaN. | One document must have one meaning and one hash. | RFC 8785 JCS (adds a dependency; our documents need no float canonicalisation beyond Python's repr). | — |
| 5 | 2026-11-17 | Statistics: exact one-sided sign test per deciding KPI; paired bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seeded by the trusted job, seed recorded) for the lower bound a bounding KPI is checked against. MDE stated before the run as ceil(log2(1/alpha)) of N cases. No extend-while-undecided rule, so no sequential correction. | Distribution-free, exact, small.
