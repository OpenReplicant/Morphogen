# Interview with Sian Hollis, managing director (2026-11-21, 50 minutes, by video)

*(Made up for Morphogen's demo engagement. Sian Hollis is a made-up person. Transcript tidied; the interviewer's
questions in bold.)*

**What would make this engagement worth it for you?**

Honestly, I want to know where the money goes. We're busier than we've ever been and the bank balance doesn't
show it. Last year we turned over four hundred thousand and I took home less than in 2022. Something's leaking and I can't see
what.

**Where do you suspect?**

Quotes, partly. Owen's calculator is the same one we've had for years. Prices have gone up, Lindale put their
carcasses up last summer, I know that. Whether Owen changed the sheet I couldn't tell you. I think he didn't.
And I'm sure we lose jobs because we're slow to quote. People tell us. "Oh, we went with someone who got back to
us." The designers are doing surveys, designs, quotes and chasing, all of it, and the quote is the bit that waits.

**How long should a quote take?**

The handbook says five working days. I'd love three. A national chain will do it in the showroom on the day,
but that's a different kind of kitchen.

**What must not change?**

The workshop. We make our own doors and that's why people come to us. And I won't have the fitters on
something they hate; they're the face of the firm in people's houses. Whatever we do has to work on their
Android phones and it has to be simple. Also the margin: 35% on cost is the number the bank and I agreed and I
want to actually get it, not change it.

**Who decides on changes?**

Me, with Mark for anything in the workshop or fitting. Dee runs the office and she should have a say on
anything she'll be typing into. We don't have an IT person. Owen is the nearest thing.

**Anything about fitting?**

The whiteboard works until it doesn't. Twice this year two pairs turned up at the same house and one job had
nobody. Quartz is a pain: Stonecraft need ten days after templating, so fitting slips if anyone forgets to book
the template. And remakes. Owen and Priya measure with a tape and a laser and a pencil, and every so often a
cabinet's wrong and we make it again. Mark says it's one job in five. That's a day of somebody's time, every
time.

**And the money coming in?**

Dee invoices when the fitter tells her it's signed off. Sometimes that's the same day, sometimes it's a fortnight
because nobody told her. People pay in a few weeks. I'd like that tightened but it's not the big thing.

**What would "better" look like in a year?**

Quotes out in three days. The margin we think we're charging is the one we get. Fewer remakes. And I stop
finding out about problems from a customer.

**Budget?**

For software, a few hundred a month if it earns it. For a project, if it pays back inside a year, I'll find it.
I'd rather not hire anyone just to run a system.

**Anything off limits for us to look at?**

No. You've got the job sheet. The accounts are with the bookkeeper; ask Dee if you need anything from Xero.
