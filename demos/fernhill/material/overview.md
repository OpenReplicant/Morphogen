# Fernhill Kitchens: who we are

*(Made up for Morphogen's demo engagement. No such firm exists.)*

Fernhill Kitchens designs, builds and fits kitchens in and around a market town in the English Midlands. We were
founded in 2009 by Sian Hollis, a joiner, and we have grown to 11 people. We make our own cabinet doors and
frames in our workshop and buy in carcasses (the cabinet boxes) and worktops.

## What we sell

- Kitchens from about £7,000 to £25,000 fitted, in two ranges: **Standard** (painted MDF doors, bought-in
  carcasses) and **Premium** (solid timber doors, heavier carcasses).
- Worktops in laminate, oak or quartz. Quartz is templated and cut by our supplier, Stonecraft, after our
  cabinets are in.
- Design, supply and fit. We do not supply appliances; customers buy their own and we fit them.
