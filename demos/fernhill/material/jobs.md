# The job sheet export (`jobs.csv`)

*(Made up for Morphogen's demo engagement.)* Exported by Dee from `jobs.xlsx` on 2026-11-20: every enquiry that
reached a survey from January 2025. Dates are ISO dates; money is pounds, before VAT.

| Column | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| job_id | our job number |
| enquiry_date, source | when and how the enquiry came in |
| designer | who surveyed, designed and quoted |
| survey_date, quote_sent_date | the survey visit; the day the quote PDF was emailed |
| cabinets, range, worktop_material, worktop_m | what was quoted (the calculator's four inputs) |
| quoted_price | the price quoted |
| outcome, lost_reason | won, lost or pending; for lost jobs, what the customer said, when they said |
| deposit_date | the deposit paid |
| fit_planned_start, fit_start, fit_end | the fitting week first written on the whiteboard; when fitting actually began and was signed off |
| remade_cabinets, remake_cause | cabinets made or ordered again, and why |
| actual_materials | supplier bills coded to the job in Xero (the bookkeeper has done this since January 2025) |
| actual_labour_hours | workshop and fitting hours from the weekly timesheets |
| final_invoice_date, paid_date | the 60% balance invoiced, and paid |

Blank means not yet, or not applicable.
